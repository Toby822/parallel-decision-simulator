RESEARCH NOTE · SPECIFICATION 1.0

What this simulator actually models

An explicitly synthetic, event-driven market-making laboratory. It is designed to test hypotheses and surface execution trade-offs—not to estimate real exchange profitability.

Event tape & information

A latent, persistent information state drives the sign of aggressive order flow and part of the next-price movement. Strategies share that market tape. The flow-aware policy only uses a lagged exponentially weighted average of observed past flow, never the latent variable or next price. Each trial uses seeded xorshift32 draws and Box–Muller normal innovations.

Order fills & quoting

Quotes are placed around a potentially stale reference mid-price. A distance- and latency-dependent Bernoulli fill rule approximates competition for flow. The model includes inventory caps and a per-fill fee. It does not reproduce matching-engine priority, cancellations, all resting depth, order routing or network jitter. The depth ladder is illustrative, not the input to matching.

Strategies

Fixed: fixed distance around the reference mid. Inventory skew: moves quote centre against accumulated inventory. Flow-aware: adds a shift and widening driven by observed signed order flow, alongside inventory skew.

P&L accounting

Marked equity = cash + inventory × mid. A fill contributes signed (mid − quote) minus fee to spread capture. Open inventory then gains or loses from later mid moves. Consequently, mean net P&L = mean spread capture + mean inventory P&L − mean fees. A 20-event markout measures signed post-fill movement and is diagnostic; do not subtract it again from net P&L.

Inference & limitations

Paths are independent synthetic trials. The 95% interval is a normal approximation to the uncertainty in sample mean P&L. It does not establish alpha, live fill rates, robust market impact or strategy viability. Scenarios are not estimates of a specific asset. Explore parameter sensitivity and reproducibility before drawing a conclusion.
